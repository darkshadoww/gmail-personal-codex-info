Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026.

This private utility accesses the owner’s Gmail profile, message identifiers, labels and message headers using the gmail.metadata scope. It uses this information to receive Gmail push events, deduplicate delivery and wake a dedicated personal Codex conversation. The watcher cannot read message bodies, send email or change mailbox labels.

The owner’s separately authorized Gmail connector allows Codex to read the actual email and perform the owner-authorized triage workflow. Relevant existing Codex conversations may receive metadata-only email pointers. Important-mail notifications use sanitized summaries and links through the owner’s notification channel.

Google Gmail and Pub/Sub deliver events; ngrok carries HTTPS traffic to the owner’s Mac; OpenAI Codex processes the owner-authorized triage; ntfy carries sanitized notifications. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Refresh credentials are stored in the Mac’s Keychain, short-lived tokens and operational records are access-restricted, and personal instructions and triage records are kept separate from work-account policies. Operational records are kept until the owner removes them. Access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections page; revocation stops future authenticated access. The owner can remove local records and credentials.
